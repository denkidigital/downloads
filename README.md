# Denki Digital: downloads

The installers for Denki Digital's instruments are attached to this repository's releases. Each
instrument's page, manual and install guide are at [www.denkidigital.com](https://www.denkidigital.com).

- Windows installers (.exe) are signed as Denki Digital Limited. Mac installers (.pkg, for Apple
  silicon and Intel) are signed and notarised by Apple.
- **Hotaru 240** and **Tsukumo 99** are free to use, including for commercial music, under the
  [Denki Digital licence agreement](https://www.denkidigital.com/licence/) (section 5).
- Every other instrument can be played in full before you buy it: until a licence unlocks it, it
  rests for two seconds each minute. The licence comes with your order.
- They are not open source. Please don't sell, rent or bundle them, or offer them for download
  anywhere else. Linking to their pages on denkidigital.com is always welcome.
