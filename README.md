# Denki Digital: free downloads

The installers for Denki Digital's free instruments, **Hotaru 240** and **Tsukumo 99**, are
attached to this repository's releases. Their pages, manuals and install guide are at
[www.denkidigital.com](https://www.denkidigital.com).

- Windows installers are signed as Denki Digital Limited. The Mac versions follow.
- The instruments are free to use, including for commercial music, under the
  [Denki Digital licence agreement](https://www.denkidigital.com/licence/) (section 5).
- They are free to download, not open source. Please don't sell, rent or bundle them, or offer them
  for download anywhere else. Linking to their pages on denkidigital.com is always welcome.
